# Support

## Community support

Use GitHub Issues for reproducible product defects and feature proposals. Include the
UnoRAG version or commit, deployment mode, sanitized logs, expected behavior and a
minimal reproduction. Use `SECURITY.md` for vulnerabilities.

Community support is best effort. Public issues must not contain customer documents,
questions, answers, prompts, credentials, private endpoints or unredacted traces.

## Professional services

Architecture review, private deployment, migration, identity and storage integration,
RAG evaluation and tuning, custom development, training, incident response and SLA
support may be arranged separately. Paying for services does not unlock a different
source edition; it pays for delivery, expertise and response commitments.
