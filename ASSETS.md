# Asset provenance

This document describes the origin and redistribution status of UnoRAG visual
assets and evaluation fixtures. The machine-readable, SHA-256-bound inventory
is [`assets/provenance.json`](./assets/provenance.json); CI rejects unregistered,
removed, or modified assets.

The UnoRAG maintainers completed the recorded engineering review on 2026-08-14
using repository history, generator sources, embedded file metadata and visual
inspection. This review found no third-party or customer material in scope.

## Uno identity

`public/brand/uno-mark.svg` is the native vector redraw of the maintainer-selected
D / Diagonal weave concept from the 2026-11-13 AI-assisted Uno design exploration.
The positive/reversed SVGs and PNGs, favicon tiles, Apple touch icon and
`docs/brand/uno-brand-board.svg` are generated from that one master, not embedded
concept rasters. UnoCSS was discussed as a reference for abstraction, not used as
a source of shapes or SVG paths. These project assets are distributed under
Apache-2.0 with the rest of this repository, subject to the separate brand-use
policy tracked for the stable release.

## Product visuals

The `public/product-library-workbench*.png` and `public/product-request-trace*.png`
files are raw browser captures of current UnoRAG components in light/dark themes.
They use synthetic document and diagnostic fixtures, not customer data or performance
benchmarks. The capture hides the real account footer and includes no credentials.
`pnpm exec tsx scripts/capture-product-visuals.ts` reproduces these four images against
the existing local development server; it requires private bootstrap credentials for
sign-in, intercepts business API reads with fixtures, and blocks business writes and
external calls. It must never point at a customer environment.

The 2026-11-13 D identity derivatives are generated by
`node scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs`. The former desk artwork is no longer used.

## Evaluation fixtures

Everything under `testdata/` is synthetic material authored or generated for
UnoRAG parser, chunking, retrieval, refusal and citation regression tests.
Company names, people, identifiers, prices and policies are fictional test
content. The DOCX files were generated with python-docx; the PDF fixtures were
generated with project scripts and ReportLab-compatible tooling. They are not
customer documents and are distributed under Apache-2.0.

Generated evaluation reports under `testdata/ab/_e2e_out/` are local evidence,
are ignored by Git, and are not release assets.

## UI source attribution

The state variants and scrollbar helper in `src/app/ui-variants.css` are adapted
from the MIT-licensed `shadcn@4.23.0/dist/tailwind.css`. The full copyright and
license are preserved in `NOTICE`, which is copied into every release image.
Slider, RadioGroup, Checkbox, Switch and Select are adapted from the official
shadcn/ui base-nova registry, together with Popover, Collapsible, HoverCard, Trace
and the AI Elements Message, Bubble, InputGroup and MessageScroller. Their source, retrieval date, product adaptations and
MIT attribution are recorded in `NOTICE`; Base UI and the MIT-licensed
`@shadcn/react` MessageScroller primitive supply interaction behavior.
This source code is separate from the visual/fixture hash inventory above.

## Change policy

New or changed files in the governed paths must update the provenance manifest
with origin, license and SHA-256. Material without a clear right to redistribute
must be replaced or removed before merge. This inventory records engineering
evidence and is not legal advice.
