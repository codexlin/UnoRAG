# UnoRAG Trademark Policy

Effective: 2026-08-22

UnoRAG is open source, but its source-code license and its product identity are
different rights. This policy explains how the UnoRAG name and visual identity may
be used without creating confusion about the source or endorsement of software and
services.

This is a project usage policy, not a claim that every mark listed below is
registered in every jurisdiction. Questions and permission requests should be
opened privately or publicly through the repository owner's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/codexlin) or in
[UnoRAG Discussions](https://github.com/codexlin/UnoRAG/discussions).

## Marks and stewardship

The UnoRAG project marks are stewarded by the repository maintainer, CodexLin, for
the Unobyte product family. Unless a specific asset says otherwise, the marks
covered by this policy are:

- the `UnoRAG` word mark and UnoRAG product logo;
- the shared `Uno` continuous-line symbol and official Uno brand assets in this
  repository;
- the `Unobyte` and `Unobyte.dev` names when they identify the project maintainer or
  product family; and
- official Uno product names, including reserved names documented in
  `docs/brand/uno-brand-system.md`.

Copyright in contributed code and assets remains with the applicable contributors.
Third-party names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.

## The Apache-2.0 boundary

The Apache License 2.0 grants broad rights to use, modify, and redistribute the
licensed source code. Section 6 does not grant a general license to use product
names, trademarks, service marks, or logos, except for reasonable and customary use
that describes the origin of the work and reproduces required notices.

You may therefore fork and modify UnoRAG code under Apache-2.0. That does not make a
modified distribution, hosted service, company, domain, or product an official
UnoRAG or Unobyte offering.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may, provided the statement is accurate and does not imply endorsement:

- refer to the project as `UnoRAG` in articles, documentation, academic work,
  reviews, issue reports, and compatibility statements;
- say that a separately branded product is "built with UnoRAG", "powered by
  UnoRAG", "compatible with UnoRAG", or "a fork of UnoRAG";
- redistribute an unmodified official UnoRAG release with its notices and source
  attribution intact;
- preserve package names, API identifiers, database identifiers, and other
  technical compatibility references that are reasonably necessary to run or
  integrate with the software;
- use unaltered screenshots or short screen recordings for truthful tutorials,
  reviews, bug reports, demonstrations, and news coverage; and
- link to the official repository and use the plain UnoRAG name to identify that
  destination.

Editorial screenshots may show the product UI and logo. They must not be edited or
framed in a way that falsely implies that a third-party product, service, or opinion
is official or endorsed.

## Forks and modified distributions

A fork or materially modified distribution must use a distinct primary name and
logo. It may describe itself as "based on UnoRAG" or "a fork of UnoRAG", but must not
present itself as the official UnoRAG release.

Retain copyright, license, NOTICE, and source attribution required by Apache-2.0.
Replace product-facing UnoRAG and Uno logos where their continued use would make
users reasonably believe the modified build comes from the UnoRAG project. Technical
identifiers may remain where changing them would break compatibility, provided the
distribution is clearly and prominently identified as unofficial.

## Hosted services

A service with its own distinct brand may truthfully state that it is "powered by
UnoRAG". Without written permission, do not use `UnoRAG`, `Uno`, `Unobyte`, an
official Uno product name, or a confusingly similar form as the service's primary
name, domain, application icon, marketplace listing, or social account. Names such
as "UnoRAG Cloud", "Official UnoRAG Hosting", and "UnoRAG Enterprise" imply an
official relationship and require permission.

## Uses that require permission

Written permission is required to:

- use an official Uno or UnoRAG logo as the primary identity of a modified product,
  hosted service, organization, domain, event, certification, merchandise, or
  commercial campaign;
- modify, animate, recolor, combine, or create derivative versions of official
  brand assets for public branding;
- use `official`, `certified`, `approved`, `partner`, or similar claims in a way that
  suggests project endorsement; or
- register or assert rights in a name, logo, domain, or account that is identical or
  confusingly similar to the marks covered by this policy.

Permission for one use does not authorize another use and may include attribution,
quality, or duration conditions.

## Canonical names

Use the following spelling and capitalization in public references:

- `UnoRAG`
- `Unobyte` and `Unobyte.dev`
- `UnoRAG Workspace`
- `UnoRAG Knowledge API`

Do not rewrite the project name as `Unorag`, `UNO RAG`, or `Uno Rag`. Code-level
lowercase identifiers such as the npm package name `unorag` are technical
exceptions, not alternate product names.

## No endorsement and changes

Use of a mark must not mislead users about origin, affiliation, sponsorship, or
endorsement. The project maintainer may ask that a confusing or harmful use stop and
may update this policy as the project and brand system mature. Applicable law may
provide additional nominative or fair-use rights; this policy does not limit rights
that cannot legally be limited.

# UnoRAG 商标使用政策

生效日期：2026-08-22

UnoRAG 是开源软件，但源代码许可证和产品标识属于不同权利。本政策说明如何使用 UnoRAG 名称与视觉标识，
而不让用户对软件或服务的来源、关联或背书产生混淆。

这是一份项目使用政策，并不宣称下列每个标识都已在所有国家或地区注册。问题和授权申请可通过仓库所有者的
[GitHub 主页](https://github.com/codexlin)联系，或在
[UnoRAG Discussions](https://github.com/codexlin/UnoRAG/discussions)中提出。

## 标识与管理

UnoRAG 项目标识由仓库维护者 CodexLin 代表 Unobyte 产品族管理。除非具体素材另有说明，本政策涵盖：

- `UnoRAG` 文字标识和 UnoRAG 产品 Logo；
- 本仓库中的 `Uno` 连续线图形与官方 Uno 品牌素材；
- 用于标识项目维护者或产品族的 `Unobyte` 与 `Unobyte.dev` 名称；
- `docs/brand/uno-brand-system.md` 中记录的正式或预留 Uno 产品名称。

贡献代码和素材的著作权仍归相应贡献者所有，第三方名称和 Logo 仍归各自权利人所有。

## Apache-2.0 与商标的边界

Apache License 2.0 允许广泛使用、修改和再分发受许可的源代码，但其第 6 条并未普遍授权使用产品名、
商标、服务标识或 Logo；仅允许为说明作品来源和保留必要通知而进行合理、惯常的使用。

因此，你可以依照 Apache-2.0 fork 和修改 UnoRAG 代码，但修改版、托管服务、公司、域名或产品不会因此
自动成为官方 UnoRAG 或 Unobyte 产品。

## 无需授权的使用

在描述真实且不暗示官方背书的前提下，你可以：

- 在文章、文档、学术作品、评测、问题报告和兼容性说明中引用 `UnoRAG`；
- 将具有独立品牌的产品描述为“基于 UnoRAG”“由 UnoRAG 驱动”“兼容 UnoRAG”或“UnoRAG 的 fork”；
- 在保留通知和来源归属的情况下再分发未经修改的官方 UnoRAG 版本；
- 保留运行或集成所合理需要的包名、API 标识、数据库标识及其他技术兼容性名称；
- 在真实的教程、评测、问题报告、演示和新闻报道中使用未经篡改的截图或短录屏；
- 使用纯文字 UnoRAG 名称链接到官方仓库。

编辑用途的截图可以展示产品界面与 Logo，但不得通过剪辑或包装让第三方产品、服务或观点看起来像官方内容
或得到了官方背书。

## Fork 与修改版

fork 或有实质修改的发行版必须采用不同的主名称和 Logo。它可以说明“基于 UnoRAG”或“UnoRAG 的 fork”，
但不得把自己呈现为官方 UnoRAG 发行版。

必须保留 Apache-2.0 要求的著作权、许可证、NOTICE 与来源归属。当继续使用 UnoRAG 或 Uno Logo 会让用户
合理误以为修改版来自 UnoRAG 项目时，应替换面向用户的品牌标识。若修改技术标识会破坏兼容性，则可保留，
但必须清晰、醒目地说明该发行版并非官方版本。

## 托管服务

具有独立品牌的服务可以如实说明“由 UnoRAG 驱动”。未经书面授权，不得将 `UnoRAG`、`Uno`、`Unobyte`、
官方 Uno 产品名称或易混淆形式用作服务主名称、域名、应用图标、应用市场条目或社交账号。`UnoRAG Cloud`、
“官方 UnoRAG 托管”和 `UnoRAG Enterprise` 等名称会暗示官方关系，必须事先获得授权。

## 需要授权的使用

以下用途需要书面授权：

- 将官方 Uno 或 UnoRAG Logo 用作修改版产品、托管服务、组织、域名、活动、认证、商品或商业宣传的主标识；
- 为公开品牌用途修改、制作动画、改色、组合官方素材或创建其衍生版本；
- 使用“官方”“认证”“批准”“合作伙伴”等可能暗示项目背书的表述；
- 注册或主张与本政策所涵盖标识相同或易混淆的名称、Logo、域名或账号权利。

一次授权不代表其他用途也获得授权，授权可附带归属、质量或有效期要求。

## 规范名称

公开引用请使用：`UnoRAG`、`Unobyte`、`Unobyte.dev`、`UnoRAG Workspace` 和
`UnoRAG Knowledge API`。不要把项目名写成 `Unorag`、`UNO RAG` 或 `Uno Rag`。npm 包名 `unorag`
等小写代码标识属于技术例外，不是替代产品名。

## 不背书与政策变更

标识使用不得误导用户对来源、关联、赞助或背书的判断。项目维护者可以要求停止造成混淆或损害的使用，并可
随项目与品牌系统发展更新本政策。适用法律可能赋予额外的指称性使用或合理使用权，本政策不限制依法不可限制
的权利。如中英文版本存在解释差异，以英文版本为准。
