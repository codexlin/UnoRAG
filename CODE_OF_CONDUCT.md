# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

UnoRAG contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful,
useful and safe. Be direct about technical risk without attacking people. Welcome
questions, explain decisions, credit work accurately, and respect privacy and
confidentiality.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sustained
disruption, or using project access to obtain customer data or credentials.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository activity, community channels, events and private
communications made in an official project role.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, limit participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a
participant when needed to protect the community.

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and impartially as practical. A person
named in a report must not make the final enforcement decision for that report.
